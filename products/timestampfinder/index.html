TimeStampFinder

Chrome Extension

In progress Chrome Extension YouTube

A Chrome extension that pulls timestamps out of any YouTube video, so you can jump straight to the part you actually need.

What it does

  • Scans a video and surfaces its timestamps without you scrubbing through the whole thing
  • Works from right inside Chrome, no separate tab or site needed
  • Built for long videos: tutorials, podcasts, lectures, anything where the good part is buried somewhere in the middle

Where it's at

Still actively being built and improved. What's live today is functional and being used, with more features on the way.
